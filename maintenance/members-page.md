# Building maintenance

Something broken, leaking, or not working? Not sure how to operate
something in your unit or the building? Tell the Maintenance & Warranty
Circle here.

## In an emergency

**Don't use the form. It isn't watched around the clock.**

- **Fire, smoke, the smell of gas, or anyone in danger:** call 911, then
  [[who to tell in the building]].
- **Water leaking:** turn off the water at [[where the shutoff is, or a link
  to the emergency guide]], then call [[after-hours number]].
- **No heat, no power, a stuck elevator, or a door that won't lock:** call
  [[after-hours number]].

Then report it on the form as well, so there's a record.

## Report a problem, or ask how something works

**[[Request form link]]**

Use it for repairs, defects, and questions about how to operate things in
your unit or the building. It works on a phone, tablet, or computer, and you
can add photos.

A few things that help:

- **One problem per request.** Three problems, three requests.
- **Photos**, whenever you can.
- **Say whether we may come in if you're out.** It saves a round of emails
  when someone needs to visit.
- **Report it even if it seems small.** While the building is under
  warranty, every defect that's written down is one the builder can be
  asked to fix.

## What happens next

1. **You get an email** saying your request arrived, with its number (like
   R-12). It can take up to two hours.
2. **A circle member looks at it** and takes it on.
3. **You get an email** when a visit is scheduled, and again when the work
   is done, asking whether it's fixed. If it isn't, just reply.

To add something or ask about your request, **reply to any of those
emails**. Your reply goes to the circle.

That first email also has **a link of your own** to the form, with your name
and unit already filled in. Bookmark it for next time.

## See where things stand

**[[Status page link]]**

This lists every open request in the building, with its number, where it
is, and what's happening with it. It shows a short description written by
the circle, not what you wrote, and never your name or photos.
